## This site

MeteSec currently does not run advertising trackers, behavioural analytics, account systems, or a first-party comments database. Search runs in the browser using a static site index. The site does not intentionally set marketing cookies.

## Hosting

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. As the hosting provider, GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses, timestamps, requested URLs, and browser details for security and delivery. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for its practices.

## External links and services

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Ko-fi, or other external sites take you to services with their own privacy policies. External widgets are not loaded unless they are explicitly enabled. If comments are enabled in the future, the article will load the configured discussion service and this page will be updated.

GitHub Discussions is available as an external community area. Opening or using it sends the usual connection data to GitHub. Posting requires a GitHub account and is governed by GitHub's own terms and privacy information.

## Email newsletter

MeteSec uses Brevo to manage voluntary email subscriptions. The subscription form on the Writing page is submitted directly to Brevo. It does not load Brevo JavaScript or tracking resources before you submit it.

When you subscribe, Brevo processes your email address, confirmation status, timestamps, and the technical information needed to receive and secure the request. This information is used only to manage and send the newsletter on the basis of your consent. A double-opt-in email is sent, and your subscription becomes active only after you confirm the address.

You can withdraw your consent at any time through the unsubscribe link included in newsletter emails. The data is retained for newsletter delivery and consent documentation until you unsubscribe or the information is no longer required, subject to applicable record-keeping obligations. More information is available in [Brevo's privacy policy](https://www.brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy/).

## Contact

If you contact MeteSec through an external platform, that platform processes the message under its own terms. Information you provide is used to respond to the request and is not sold.

This page will be updated when another service materially changes data processing on MeteSec.
