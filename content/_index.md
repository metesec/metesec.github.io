## Removing the black boxes

I spent years working in IT while many of the systems underneath still felt like black boxes.

MeteSec is where I make that journey visible: connecting secure engineering, offensive security, and cyber defense through practical work and deliberate reflection.

Expect technical deep dives, selected projects, commentary, and honest accounts of what worked, what did not, and what I learned along the way.

**Build systems with security in mind. Break assumptions before adversaries do. Defend with context.**
