Every article belongs to one primary editorial track:

- **Build** — creating systems with security in mind.
- **Break** — testing assumptions and understanding failure.
- **Defend** — detecting, investigating, and responding with context.
- **Journey** — documenting learning, progress, and professional development.
- **Commentary** — perspectives on security practice and the wider industry.
