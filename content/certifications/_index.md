## The Journey Behind the Badges

The individual certifications matter, but the sequence matters more. Each one gave me another piece of the map: first the foundations, then infrastructure and cloud, then security, and finally more practical ways to connect those areas.

I explore that progression in [The Long Way Around: How Certifications Taught Me to Learn IT](/posts/the-long-way-around/).
